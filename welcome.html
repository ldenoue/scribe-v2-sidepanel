Welcome to QuickScribe!

Thank you for installing QuickScribe. Here's how to get started...

  1. Go to any YouTube video
  2. Click the QuickScribe icon
  3. The sidepanel* will open and show you the transcript
  4. Login to QuickScribe if not logged in already (free account)
* If you are not on a YouTube video page, the sidepanel will not open.

Some tips about using QuickScribe:

  • Click anywhere on the text will jump to that point in the video
  • Using the timeline in the YouTube will also jump to the corresponding passage in the transcript
  • Select any text to highlight it

Access all your transcripts from the QuickScribe home page at QuickScribe Home
